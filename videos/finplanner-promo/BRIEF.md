# Brief: FinPlanner Property Showcase

## Intent
Site tour / showcase — display the property detail page on FinPlanner and explain what it does.

## Product
**FinPlanner** — AI-Powered Financial & Real Estate Platform for Karnataka, India. Combines property listings, financial planning, AI agents, leads management, marketing, marketplace, legal tools, and community features into one platform.

## Target Audience
Indian real estate buyers and professionals in Karnataka (Hubli, Bangalore, Mysore).

## Video Length
30-45 seconds

## Format
Landscape 1920×1080

## Focus Page
Properties detail page showing Sobha City property with:
- Property images and gallery
- Property details (builder, rating, location, type, configuration, area)
- Amenities list
- Pricing and cost calculator
- RERA compliance information
- Contact options for property inquiries

## Key Messaging
- Detailed property information and visualization
- AI-powered financial planning integration
- Transparent pricing with built-in calculators
- RERA verified properties
- Direct builder connectivity
- Karnataka-focused real estate solutions

## Tone
Professional, informative, trustworthy. Financial/real estate industry standards.

## Voiceover Style
Explanatory narration walking through each section of the property detail page. Clear, informative, professional.

## Music
Background music: subtle, corporate - light and professional (no narration required for this focused showcase)

## Brand Colors (from site)
- Primary: Green (#10B981) for action buttons and CTAs
- Background: White/Clean
- Text: Dark/Black  
- Accents: Blue links for navigation

## Style Preset
To be determined - will choose based on design review

## Story Structure
Demonstration: Walk through the property detail page, explaining each section and its function for users searching real estate properties.
