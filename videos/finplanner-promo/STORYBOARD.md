## Video direction

**Palette system:** Dark canvas (#0F172A) with white content cards. Indigo accent (#4F46E5) for interactive elements. Green (#10B981) for CTAs and success states. Gray text ladder: #FFFFFF → #94A3B8 → #64748B.

**Motion grammar:** VO-paced reveals — each UI element enters when the voiceover names it. Long-tail eases (power3 default). Screenshots enter with subtle scale + fade. Crossfade transitions between frames.

**Rhythm:** Frame 1 introduces the platform. Frames 2-5 showcase different aspects of the property detail page. Frame 6 shows the CTA.

**Negative list:** No browser chrome, no scrollbars, no floating decorative shapes. Screenshots are cropped to content only. No purple-blue AI gradients.
