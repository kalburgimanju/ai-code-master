# Brief: FinPlanner Sobha City Property Showcase

## Intent
Site showcase — display the Sobha City property detail page on FinPlanner and explain what it does.

## Product
**FinPlanner** — AI-Powered Financial & Real Estate Platform for Karnataka, India. Displays property listings with comprehensive details including builder information, property specifications, pricing, and AI-powered financial planning integration.

## Target Audience
Indian real estate buyers interested in premium residential properties in Karnataka, particularly those seeking AI-integrated financial planning tools.

## Video Length
30 seconds

## Format
Landscape 1920×1080

## Focus Page
Sobha City property detail page showcasing:
- Property images and gallery
- Builder information (Sobha Ltd, 4.3 star rating from 128 reviews)
- Property details (Hubli location, 15-30 sq ft range, 2-3 BHK options)
- Amenities list and features
- Pricing calculator and cost breakdown
- RERA compliance information
- Contact options for property inquiries

## Key Messaging
- Premium real estate properties in Karnataka region
- AI-powered financial integration for property purchases
- Transparent pricing with detailed cost breakdown
- RERA verified properties for buyer protection
- Professional builder portfolio and reputation
- Built-in financial planning tools

## Tone
Professional, informative, trustworthy. Financial/real estate industry standards.

## Voiceover Style
Technical yet approachable walkthrough of property details and financial options. Clear, informative narration explaining each section.

## Music
Background music: subtle, professional - light and refined

## Brand Colors (from site)
- Primary: Green (#10B981) for action buttons and CTAs
- Background: Dark slate (#0F172A)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF) with shades of gray for hierarchy
- Accents: Blue (#4F46E5) for interactive elements

## Style Preset
Blue Professional — clean, consulting-style presentation with refined cards and no shadows

## Story Structure
Demonstration: Walk through the Sobha City property detail page, explaining each section and its functionality for prospective buyers.
